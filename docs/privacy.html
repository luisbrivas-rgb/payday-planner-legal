🔒 Payday Planner does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

📋 Overview

Payday Planner ("the App") is a personal finance tool designed to help individuals take control of their budget — one payday at a time. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

By using Payday Planner, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

💾 Data Storage

All data you enter into Payday Planner — including your net pay, bills, payments, and budget information — is stored locally on your device only, using your browser's localStorage or the app's local storage.

  • We do not use any external servers or databases.
  • We do not transmit your data over the internet.
  • Your data is never sent to Anthropic, Apple, or any third party.

🚫 Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, address)
  • Financial account numbers or banking credentials
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or analytics
  • Usage statistics or crash reports
  • Cookies or tracking technologies

🔐 Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your information depends on the security of your device. We recommend:

  • Using a device passcode or biometric lock
  • Regularly exporting a backup using the app's built-in Export feature
  • Keeping your device's operating system up to date

👶 Children's Privacy

Payday Planner is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has entered personal information, please contact us.

🔄 Third-Party Services

Payday Planner does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. No data is shared with any outside party.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Questions about this policy?

Contact us at support@payday-manager.com
